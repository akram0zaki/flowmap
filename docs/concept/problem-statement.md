People responsible for coordinating engineering and delivery work often face a recurring set of challenges:

- New demand must be weighed against work already in progress. Capacity is difficult to estimate when planned initiatives compete with operational support, incidents, maintenance, and other unplanned work. As a result, commitments can be made without a clear view of what teams can realistically take on.

- Information about work is distributed across specialist systems, documents, conversations, and informal notes. Each source may be useful in isolation, but there is rarely a lightweight view that brings the relevant work, dependencies, decisions, and constraints together.

- With many initiatives underway and other ideas still taking shape, it is easy to lose track of what needs attention next. People spend time switching between contexts and maintaining personal lists instead of seeing which topics require action to stay on course.

- When work and dependencies are fragmented, bottlenecks, competing priorities, and emerging delivery risks are difficult to spot early.

Flowmap is intended to address this gap with a lightweight visual workspace for understanding a delivery portfolio. It should make work, capacity, dependencies, and attention points easy to explore and update through an approachable graphical interface that works for both business and technical users.

The tool should be simple to run and suitable for environments where centrally hosted applications or new services are not always available. A portable desktop or local web application, backed by a shared file-based workspace when needed, can support collaboration while fitting within existing organizational and security constraints.
